My experience

When I was starting over in the United States, I was raising my child while building my own insurance team from the ground up.

Before coming to the United States, I worked in insurance in China and also led a team. In 2017, I built my own team in Hawaii, and in the first year we reached $1 million in team production. I now lead teams in both Hawaii and Las Vegas.

I want to help Chinese in the United States, ages 30 to 50, make this path into an insurance business work. From getting licensed and writing the first policy, to later leading a team of my own, I will share how I made my choices, the pitfalls I ran into, and how I adjusted when something was not working.

If you are considering entering the industry, or you already work in insurance and want to build a team of your own, I welcome you to talk with me.
